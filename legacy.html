What to expect at your size

With two songs and a few hundred listeners, your best odds are community festivals, multi-venue town festivals and smaller rock festivals. The big festivals mostly book through agents and labels.

£0–£150

Pay

Plan to play for free or close to it. Twyfest says outright it can't pay. Dart asks you to propose a fee. Research into unsigned stages puts the typical fee around £100, which rarely covers van hire and fuel. You pay your own travel and accommodation almost everywhere.

20–40 min

The slot

Expect an early-afternoon slot on a small or second stage. FOCUS Wales showcase sets are 30 minutes. Two songs won't fill that, so you'll need a set of unreleased material that's ready to play live.

Silence

Replies

The Great Escape, Sunshine Festival and Wokingham all say they can't reply to everyone. If you hear nothing, you weren't picked. The Great Escape says no reply by 31 March 2027 means no.

Live video

What helps most

DonnyFest asks for one and Sunshine won't consider entries without performance links. One well-filmed live song does more for your chances than more followers would. A BBC Introducing upload helps too, because Camper Calling's Freshly Squeezed stage books BBC Introducing acts.

Have these ready before you apply

Most forms ask for the same things. Get them sorted once and each application takes about ten minutes.

  • One live video. A single song, filmed well, with clear audio. DonnyFest and Sunshine both ask for one.
  • Spotify for Artists claimed, with a photo and a bio. This is the first thing bookers click.
  • A 280-character pitch. Teddy Rocks caps it there. Say where you're from, who you sound like and one fact that sets you apart.
  • A BBC Introducing upload. It's free, gets you local radio plays and puts you in the running for Camper Calling's Freshly Squeezed stage.
  • Public liability insurance and PRS membership. Dart asks for both. Musicians' Union membership includes insurance.
  • A shared calendar of dates you can play. Several forms ask you to confirm you're free on every day of the festival.
  • A third release before February, if you can. It shows momentum to the showcase festivals that close later.
  • A note of where you live. DonnyFest, Twyfest, Wokingham and In It Together all favour local acts, so lead with it where it fits.
